What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the website remember information about your visit.

How This Site Uses Cookies

This Site uses cookies primarily for:

  • Analytics: Google Analytics cookies help me understand how visitors use the Site (for example, which pages are most popular).

Managing Cookies

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings (for example, to block or delete them). If you disable cookies, some analytics features may not work, but you will still be able to view the Site.
